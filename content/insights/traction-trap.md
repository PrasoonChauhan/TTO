Every startup we meet in orbit two has done something genuinely hard. They built a product people pay for. They found the first customers. They survived.

And almost all of them are trying to scale with the same playbook that got them there.

## The three orbits

**Orbit one** is the idea: a founder circling a conviction worth building.

**Orbit two** is traction: prototype, first customers, first signal. It runs on founder energy, warm intros and a willingness to do whatever it takes.

**Orbit three** is scale. And scale has a cruel property: the things that created traction start working against you.

## Why the playbook breaks

In orbit two, the founder is the strategy. They sell every deal, they write the website copy at midnight, they know every customer by name. It works because one brilliant person can hold the whole company in their head.

In orbit three, that same pattern becomes the ceiling.

- Deals stall when the founder is busy.
- New hires cannot sell what lives only in the founder's head.
- Marketing has no clear story to amplify, so it produces noise.
- Decisions get made on instinct because nobody built the dashboards.

> Momentum got you here. It will not get you further on its own.

## How to tell which orbit you are in

Ask three questions:

1. **Could your team close a deal this month without you?**
2. **Can three different employees describe what you do in the same sentence?**
3. **Do you know which channel produced last quarter's best customers?**

Three "yes" answers and you are already in orbit three. Any "no" marks the gap to close first.

## What to change first

Start with positioning. It sounds abstract, but it is the foundation for everything else: the sales narrative, the website, the content plan and the metrics that matter all flow from a clear answer to *what you do, for whom, and why you*.

Then build the systems in order: a sales story your team can tell, a website that sells while you sleep, and a small set of numbers everyone watches.

Escaping orbit takes clarity. Everything else is execution.
