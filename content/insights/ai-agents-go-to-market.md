Something quiet happened to the top of the funnel this year. The founders we work with started hearing a new answer to "how did you find us?": *"I asked an AI assistant for options."*

It is not a fringe behaviour any more. Buyers are using AI assistants to do most of their early research: defining the problem, listing vendors, comparing pricing and drafting the shortlist. By the time they land on your website, the decision is half made.

## What actually changed

Search engines ranked pages. Assistants **summarise companies**. That is a different job, and it rewards different things.

- **Clarity over keywords.** An assistant can only recommend you if it can explain, in one sentence, what you do and who you do it for.
- **Specifics over slogans.** "We help teams move faster" gives a model nothing to work with. "Payroll for remote teams in 40 countries" does.
- **Evidence over claims.** Case studies, numbers and named customers are what models quote when they justify a recommendation.

> If an AI cannot describe your company in one sentence, it will describe a competitor instead.

## The new top of funnel

For a startup between traction and scale, this is good news. Big brands win on budget. Assistants reward precision, and precision is cheap.

### 1. Make your positioning machine-readable

Your homepage headline, your About page and your product pages should all state the same three facts: **what you do, who it is for, and what makes you different**. Consistently, in plain language, on pages a crawler can read.

### 2. Publish answers, not announcements

Write the pages your buyers ask assistants about: comparisons, pricing logic, implementation guides, "how to choose" articles. Each one is a chance to be the source an assistant cites.

### 3. Give every claim a number

"Faster onboarding" becomes "onboarding in 9 days, down from 6 weeks". Numbers survive summarisation. Adjectives do not.

## How to measure it

Most analytics tools still lump AI traffic into "direct" or "referral". Two simple fixes:

1. Create a segment for referrers like chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai and gemini.google.com.
2. Add one question to every demo form: *"Where did you first hear about us?"* Include "An AI assistant" as an option.

Within a quarter you will know whether assistants are sending you buyers, and which pages they are reading.

## What to do this quarter

| Week | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Ask four assistants to describe your company and your category. Note the gaps. |
| 2 | Rewrite your homepage and About page to state what, who and why in plain language. |
| 3 | Publish one comparison or "how to choose" page your buyers actually need. |
| 4 | Add AI referral tracking and the "where did you hear about us" question. |

The companies that win this shift will not be the loudest. They will be the easiest to explain.
